[h1]Awakening Crystal Converter[/h1]
Late-game convenience, less grinding, and a very large electricity bill.
Advanced wafer fabrication for crystal conversion (X). Giving your hands a break while your base's power grid works overtime (O).
Convenience comes at a price: level 74 and an extremely demanding power budget.
Exchange Awakening Crystals between all nine elements at a [b]1:1 ratio[/b]. With power available, each conversion takes one second. Redistribute your late-game stockpile without creating extra crystals.

[h2]Warning: enormous operating power consumption[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Idle: 36,000 per second.[/b] The building draws power even without an exchange order.
[*][b]Operating total: 576,000 per second.[/b] This includes idle consumption; do not add it again. Equivalent to 192 devices drawing 3,000 per second each.
[*]Power loss pauses conversion; restoring power resumes it. Completing or cancelling an order returns the machine to idle consumption.
[/list]
[b]Author's test: a Pal with maximum work speed and maximum electricity work suitability, paired with a Large Power Generator, could only just keep up.[/b]
That base also contained 20 oil extractors drawing 3,000/s each, both Pal Resource Factory buildings, and other vanilla buildings. This is a whole-base observation, not a standalone power guarantee for this machine. Stored power can drop rapidly while the Pal eats or leaves its post.
Prepare substantial generation and storage. An Ancient Civilization Power Generator or multiple generators are potential upgrades; the actual balance depends on Pal capabilities, work uptime, and total base demand.

[h2]Unlock and use[/h2]
[list]
[*]Normal Technology, level 74, 3 Technology Points, beside the Ancient Civilization Relic Converter.
[*]Materials: 150 World Tree Ingots, 150 mysterious wood (Wood_WorldTree), 90 Ancient Civilization Parts, and 60 Ancient Civilization Cores.
[*]Choose the element you want to receive, then select the crystals to spend and the quantity.
[*]No working or stored Pal required; the machine uses base electricity directly.
[/list]

[h2]Languages and tested scope[/h2]
17 interface languages follow the game language. Fully restart after changing languages. Names, descriptions, and exchange menus display one language at a time; lettering on the machine remains unchanged.
Tested on Windows Steam Palworld 1.0.4.102642. Single-player exchange, partial cancellation and collection, completed orders across restarts, demolition returns, power interruption/resumption, and power balance have been accepted in author testing. All 17 languages passed local completeness checks; their in-game layouts have not each been verified. Multiplayer and dedicated servers are unverified.
Before uninstalling, back up your save, collect your items, and dismantle the mod's buildings while the mod is still enabled.

[h1]屬性晶石轉換機[/h1]
大後期降肝專用。高階晶圓製程協助晶石轉換（X），把你的肝省下來，交給基地電網加班（O）。
方便歸方便，代價還是要付：74 級才開放，還得供得起這台吃電怪獸。
將九種屬性的覺醒晶石以 1：1 互相兌換。供電充足時每秒完成一顆，方便調整後期晶石庫存；不會憑空增加晶石數量。

[h2]注意：運轉耗電量驚人[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]待機耗電：36,000／秒。[/b] 即使沒有兌換訂單，建築仍會持續增加基地用電負擔。
[*][b]運轉總耗電：576,000／秒。[/b] 這是工作時的總值，不再另加待機耗電；相當於 192 台每秒耗電 3,000 的設備。
[*]停電會暫停兌換，恢復供電後繼續。完成或取消訂單後回到待機耗電。
[/list]
[b]作者實測：滿工作速度、滿發電適應性的帕魯，搭配大型發電機也僅勉強支撐。[/b]
這是在同一基地另有 20 座各耗電 3,000／秒的油田、帕魯資源工廠的兩台設備及其他原版建築的情況下測試，並非這台機器單獨運作的供電保證。帕魯吃飯或離開發電崗位時，儲備電量可能快速下降。
請準備充足發電與蓄電能力；更高階的古代文明發電機或多機供電，是可考慮的升級方向。實際收支仍取決於帕魯能力、工作狀態與整個基地的負載。

[h2]解鎖與使用[/h2]
[list]
[*]普通科技 74 級，消耗 3 點科技點；位於古代文明遺物轉換器旁。
[*]建材：帕魯樹晶錠 ×150、神秘木材 ×150、古代文明部件 ×90、古代文明核心 ×60。
[*]選擇想取得的屬性，再選要消耗的晶石與數量，開始兌換。
[*]不需要派工或封裝帕魯；直接使用基地供電。
[/list]

[h2]語言與測試範圍[/h2]
